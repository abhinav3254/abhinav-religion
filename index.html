Day by Day

3-Day Itinerary

Day One Kathmandu Arrival
08:00
Land at Tribhuvan Airport
Check into Thamel. Exchange currency, pick up Ncell SIM.
10:30
Swayambhunath (Monkey Temple)
Climb 365 steps for a panoramic valley view. Spot resident macaques.
13:00
Dal Bhat Lunch — Thamel
Unlimited Nepali thali. The real deal, not tourist-watered.
15:00
Pashupatinath Temple
Watch Aarti on the Bagmati ghats. Sadhus, incense, eternal flame.
18:30
Boudhanath Kora at Dusk
Evening circumambulation. Rooftop dinner overlooking the stupa.
Day Two Bhaktapur + Nagarkot
06:00
Drive to Bhaktapur
Beat crowds to the medieval city. Explore Durbar Square before 9 AM.
09:30
Nyatapola Temple + Potter's Square
Nepal's tallest pagoda. Watch potters shape clay on hand-turned wheels.
11:30
Juju Dhau (King Curd)
Mandatory stop — legendary sweet yoghurt served in a clay bowl.
14:00
Drive up to Nagarkot
32 km scenic hill drive. Check into hillside resort. Cool mountain air.
17:30
Himalayan Sunset
Watch alpenglow paint Ganesh Himal, Langtang & Everest golden.
Day Three Nagarkot Sunrise + Pokhara
05:00
🌄 Nagarkot Sunrise
The moment the trip is built around. Himalayas glow flamingo-pink. Don't miss it.
08:00
Fly / Drive to Pokhara
25-min flight (recommended ✈) or scenic 7-hr Prithvi Highway drive.
12:00
Phewa Lake Boating
Row to Tal Barahi island temple. The Annapurna massif (8,091 m) reflects perfectly in the still water on clear mornings.
15:30
Lakeside Shopping
Pashminas, Thangka art, singing bowls. Negotiate hard.
19:00
Farewell Dinner
Buff momo + apple brandy. Toast to Nepal. Fly home or onward.

Before You Pack

Traveller Tips

🛂
No Visa Needed
Indians don't need a visa for Nepal. Just your Aadhar / passport at TIA. Zero fee, smooth entry.
💵
INR Widely Accepted
₹100, ₹200, ₹500 accepted at 1.6x rate. Keep NPR for small shops. ATMs in Thamel work with Indian cards.
🌤
Best Season
Oct–Nov for crystal mountain views. Mar–May for rhododendrons. Monsoon (Jun–Sep) = poor visibility.
🕉
Temple Etiquette
Remove shoes. Dress modestly (shoulders + knees covered). Walk clockwise around stupas. No pointing feet at idols.
🚕
Hire a Private Jeep
~₹7,000–9,000 for 2 days covers Ktm → Bhaktapur → Nagarkot. Far more flexible than shared taxis. Negotiate the night before.
🍜
Must Eat
Buff momo, Dal Bhat (unlimited refills!), Juju Dhau in Bhaktapur, Sel Roti, apple brandy. Avoid raw salads.
📱
Get Ncell SIM
Buy at TIA for ~NPR 100. Fast 4G in cities. Coverage weakens in Nagarkot hills — download maps offline.
🎒
Pack for Altitude
Layers for Nagarkot (can drop to 8°C at night). Good walking shoes essential. Sunscreen — UV is intense at 2,000+ m.
