1
Aapki aankhein Jab aap khush hoti hain, aapki aankhon mein ek alag hi chamak aati hai — woh chamak mere din ka sabse khoobsurat hissa hai.
2
Aapka confidence Aap jo sochti hain, seedha keh deti hain. Koi bakwaas nahi, koi drama nahi. Yahi baat aapko sabse alag banati hai.
3
Aapki hasi Aapki hasi sunke lagta hai sab theek ho jayega. Seriously — koi bhi tension ho, aapki ek hassi sab khatam kar deti hai.
4
Aapki care Aap chillaati bhi hain toh isliye kyunki fikar hoti hai. Naraaz hoti hain toh bhi woh pyaar ka hi ek roop hai — aur main samajhta hoon.
5
Aapka dil Aap chahe kitni bhi gusse mein hon — andar se aap bahut soft hain. Aur yahi aapko itna khaas banata hai.
6
Meri duniya Aapki ek "good morning" se mera din shuru hota hai aur aapki "good night" se khatam. Aap ek routine nahi — zaroorat hain meri.
7
Aapka judgment Aap kabhi bhi bekar cheez pe time waste nahi karti. Smart hain aap — aur honestly thoda intimidating bhi — but I love that.
8
Aapki stubborn side Aap naraaz hoti hain toh properly hoti hain — half-heartedly nahi. Yahi side bhi pyaari lagti hai aap mein, I'm not even kidding.
9
Mera better version Aapke saath rehke main better banta hoon. Aapko impress karna chahta hoon — isliye try karta rehta hoon. Aap meri motivation hain.
10
Bas aap — sirf aap Koi reason nahi chahiye honestly. Aapki ID dekh ke dil thoda zyada dhak dhak karta hai — aur yahi kaafi hai.
